How the score works. Each item is marked the way an evaluator would: met or not, or 0, 1 or 2 for quality. The tool adds the raw points and converts them with the tables in the 2026 평가편람 for existing centers, including the 필수 조건 that zero a year or an indicator. It is a self-estimate: the evaluators' judgement of quality decides the real score.

Years and files. Columns 2023, 2024 and 2025 are the three years a 2026 evaluation covers. "Now" means the current state is checked. For per-child records, three files are checked: two the center chooses and one picked at random. The tool scores your two best files plus your weakest file, which is the worst case.

Interpretations used. The handbook does not spell these out, so the tool takes the stricter reading. A program year that fails its 필수 조건 earns nothing for that year. Where any child's file fails a 필수 조건, the whole indicator scores 0. For 종사자 의무교육, an item counts only if all three years are marked. For 후원금, a failed condition costs all 4 points, although fixing it before the 지도점검 can reduce the loss to 1 or 2. Confirm these with your 시도지원단.

Sharing. This page holds one center's checklist. Everyone who can open it with Contributor access or higher sees and edits the same marks. Another center needs its own copy of the page.

Priority view. It sorts every item that is not at full marks into four groups. "Do on time" holds records for years that are still open. "Fix now" holds yes-or-no items checked as they stand on the day. "Raise quality" holds graded items. "Cannot be fixed" holds past years and penalties. The number beside an item is what the score gains if that item alone reaches full marks; where it says 0, the item only earns points together with others in the same indicator. Do not backdate records: the handbook penalises false entries.

Format. "Strict" means the form is fixed for you: a statutory or official form, a 희망이음 or evaluation-system screen, or a paper someone else issues (a 시·군·구 notice, a certificate, a receipt). Use it as given and leave no field blank. "Standard" means the 운영 매뉴얼 has a sample form or the 평가편람 names items that must appear; your own layout is fine as long as those items are there. "Format-free" means any record that shows it happened counts, such as minutes, a memo or photos. The short note under each label says why. A label with an arrow (→) opens the recommended form for that document on its own page: the sample from the 운영 매뉴얼 or the 평가편람, an outline where the sample is a long set of rules, or the writing guide's list where the sample page was not available.
